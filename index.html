FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is NovaFlow?

NovaFlow is an AI-powered customer communication system designed to help businesses respond to inquiries, capture leads, follow up and support appointment booking.

Does NovaFlow replace my receptionist?

NovaFlow is designed to support your existing business operations by handling repetitive customer conversations and helping your team respond more consistently.

Can the AI answer questions about my business?

Yes. Your system can be configured with information such as your services, pricing, hours, policies and frequently asked questions.

Can NovaFlow help with appointments?

NovaFlow can be configured to support your booking workflow and guide customers toward the appropriate appointment process.

Do I need to change my entire business system?

Not necessarily. The goal is to build NovaFlow around the tools and processes your business already uses whenever practical.

How do I get started?

Book a free consultation. We'll discuss your current customer communication process and determine what NovaFlow can automate for your business.
