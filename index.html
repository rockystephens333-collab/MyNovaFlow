Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Before Getting Started?

Here's what businesses usually want to know.

What exactly is MyNovaFlow?

MyNovaFlow is an AI-powered customer communication and business automation service designed to help service-based businesses respond to customers, organize leads, follow up and support booking workflows.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

The $500 setup fee covers the customization and setup of your system. The recurring monthly service then includes a 7-day free trial. Unless canceled before the trial ends, the monthly subscription begins at $297.

Can the AI answer questions about my business?

Yes. Your system can be configured using your services, pricing, hours, policies, frequently asked questions and other business information.

Can MyNovaFlow help customers book appointments?

Yes, depending on your booking platform and the integrations available. We review your current setup during onboarding.

Do I have to replace my current booking system?

Not necessarily. MyNovaFlow is designed to work around existing business processes whenever the platform and integration options allow it.

Do I need to understand AI or automation?

No. You provide the business information. MyNovaFlow handles the system configuration.

Does MyNovaFlow guarantee more revenue?

No specific revenue, sales or booking results are guaranteed. MyNovaFlow provides technology designed to support customer communication and business processes.
