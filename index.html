Frequently Asked Questions

What is MyNovaFlow Technologies?

MyNovaFlow Technologies provides AI-powered customer communication and automation systems for service-based businesses.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

The $500 setup fee covers building and configuring your system. After setup, the recurring MyNovaFlow service includes a 7-day free trial. Unless canceled before the trial ends, the monthly subscription begins at $297.

Can the AI answer questions about my business?

Yes. Your AI can be configured with information such as services, pricing, policies, business hours and frequently asked questions.

Can MyNovaFlow help customers book appointments?

MyNovaFlow can support booking workflows. Exact functionality depends on the booking platform and integrations available to your business.

Do I have to replace my existing booking system?

Not necessarily. We review your current setup and determine the best supported way to integrate MyNovaFlow into your existing process.

Do I need to understand AI?

No. We handle the technical configuration. You provide the business information needed to customize the system.

Does MyNovaFlow guarantee more revenue?

No specific revenue or booking results are guaranteed. MyNovaFlow provides technology designed to improve customer communication and business processes.
